Legal Validity

Sub-ULPINs generated here are simulation-only. Final cadastral partition requires a registered Mutation deed (Batwara Patra) under State Revenue Code.

Area Formula

Sub-plot Area = (Total Area × Ratio) ÷ Sum of all ratios.
1 Hectare = 2.471 Acres = 100 Guntha

Tip

Ratios don't need to add up to 100. Use any numbers (e.g. 2:1 = 2,1). The system auto-normalises and calculates exact shares.
