SECURITY_DEFENSIVE_POLICY

This system adheres to the Open Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) security standards:

  • Session timeout fixed to 30 minutes with forced re-authentication.
  • Zero hardcoded keys: Fernet keys and secret salts pulled from environment variables.
  • All spatial uploads reprojected to EPSG:4326 and validated for polygon topological closure.
  • Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS) restricted to authenticated institutional clients.
