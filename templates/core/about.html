ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Integrated GIS Digital Public Infrastructure

A unified digital governance framework designed to eliminate land record tampering, double-selling fraud, and boundary disputes in rural India.

1. Cadastral Spatial GIS

BhuRaksha digitizes physical village maps (Aks Shajra) into clean EPSG:4326 GeoJSON polygons. Each plot is indexed by a 14-digit Unique Land Parcel Identification Number (ULPIN).

2. Cryptographic Integrity

Every cadastral parcel stores a SHA-256 fingerprint of its boundary coordinates and ownership shares. Any unauthorized backend modification breaks the hash chain immediately.

3. Mutation & Verification

Transparent end-to-end digital mutations (Dakhil-Kharij) for sale, inheritance, and partition. Provides publicly verifiable Record of Rights (Fard) certificates with QR codes.

Technical Architecture & Stack

  • Backend Framework: Python 3.13, Django 6.1
  • Web GIS Engine: Leaflet.js, CartoDB Light/Dark Tiles
  • Spatial Computations: Shapely Geometry Engine (No GDAL req)
  • Database: SQLite (Dev) / PostgreSQL PostGIS Ready
  • Security Standard: Fernet AES-128 PII Encryption
  • Audit Trail: Append-Only SHA-256 Hash Chain
  • Pilot Cluster: Phagwara (Kot Rani & Khera Dehat), Punjab
  • Total Digitized Parcels: 50 Cadastral Plots
