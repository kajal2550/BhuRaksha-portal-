Core Architectural Pillars

Three integrated layers that make BhuRaksha tamper-proof and verifiable

1. Cadastral Spatial GIS

BhuRaksha digitizes physical village maps (Aks Shajra) into clean EPSG:4326 GeoJSON polygons. Each plot is indexed by a 14-digit Unique Land Parcel Identification Number (ULPIN).

Leaflet.js · GeoJSON · EPSG:4326
2. Cryptographic Integrity

Every cadastral parcel stores a SHA-256 fingerprint of its boundary coordinates and ownership shares. Any unauthorized backend modification breaks the hash chain immediately.

SHA-256 · Fernet AES-128 · Append-Only Audit
3. Mutation & Verification

Transparent end-to-end digital mutations (Dakhil-Kharij) for sale, inheritance, and partition. Provides publicly verifiable Record of Rights (Fard) certificates with QR codes.

QR Verification · RoR Certificates · ULPIN
